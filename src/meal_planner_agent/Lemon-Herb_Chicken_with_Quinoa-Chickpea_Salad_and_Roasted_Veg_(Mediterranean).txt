Lemon-Herb Chicken with Quinoa-Chickpea Salad and Roasted Veg (Mediterranean)

🛒 Ingredients
• Chicken breast, skinless, raw — 210 gm
• Quinoa, dry — 50 gm
• Chickpeas, canned low-sodium, rinsed — 75 gm
• Broccoli florets — 250 gm
• Zucchini — 200 gm
• Cucumber — 150 gm
• Cherry tomatoes — 150 gm
• Extra virgin olive oil — 2.5 tbsp
• Lemon — x2
• Garlic cloves — x3
• Dried oregano — 2 tsp
• Fresh parsley — 15 gm
• Salt — 0.25 tsp
• Black pepper — 0.5 tsp
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat oven to 200C (400F). Line a tray with parchment.
2. Bake the marinated chicken for 20-22 minutes, until it reaches 74C (165F). Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
3. Toss broccoli and zucchini with 1 tbsp olive oil and pepper. Roast on a second tray for 15-18 minutes.
4. Toss quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes and parsley with the juice of 1 lemon and the remaining 0.5 tbsp olive oil.
5. Plate by the diner portions listed. Joy gets the larger portion and Alice the smaller. Serve warm.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Lemon-herb baked chicken breast — 120 gm raw (about 90 gm cooked)
Alice: Lemon-herb baked chicken breast — 90 gm raw (about 68 gm cooked)
Joy: Quinoa-chickpea salad with cucumber, tomato and parsley — 1/2 cup quinoa + 1/4 cup chickpeas + 1 cup cucumber-tomato mix
Alice: Quinoa-chickpea salad with cucumber, tomato and parsley — 1/3 cup quinoa + 3 tbsp chickpeas + 3/4 cup cucumber-tomato mix
Joy: Garlic roasted broccoli and zucchini — 1.5 cups
Alice: Garlic roasted broccoli and zucchini — 1.25 cups
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 570 kcal, 38.0 g protein
Alice: 425 kcal, 29.0 g protein

Lemon-Yogurt Chicken with Mediterranean Chickpea Salad and Roasted Broccoli

🛒 Ingredients
• Boneless skinless chicken breast — 200 gm
• Plain unsweetened nonfat Greek yogurt — 70 gm
• No-salt-added canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained — 200 gm
• Broccoli florets — 270 gm
• Cucumber, diced — 130 gm
• Tomato, diced — 100 gm
• Red onion, finely diced — 40 gm
• Extra virgin olive oil — 24 gm
• Lemon (juice and zest) — x1
• Garlic cloves, minced — x2
• Cumin and smoked paprika blend (half and half) — 4 gm
• Fresh parsley, chopped — 10 gm
• Salt — 2 gm
• Black pepper — 1 gm
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 220C (425F). Line a tray with parchment.
2. Mix yogurt, half the lemon juice, lemon zest, 1 minced garlic clove, spice blend, 1 gm salt and pepper. Coat the chicken and marinate for 15 minutes.
3. Toss broccoli with 8 gm olive oil, the remaining garlic and a pinch of pepper. Spread it on the tray.
4. Place the chicken on the same tray. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes, until the chicken reaches 74C (165F) and the broccoli edges are browned. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes.
5. While baking, combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, red onion and parsley. Dress with 16 gm olive oil, the remaining lemon juice, 1 gm salt and pepper.
6. Slice the chicken. Plate the chicken, salad and broccoli using the serving sizes for each diner.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Lemon-yogurt baked chicken — 110 gm raw (about 85 gm cooked)
Alice: Lemon-yogurt baked chicken — 90 gm raw (about 70 gm cooked)
Joy: Mediterranean chickpea, cucumber and tomato salad — 120 gm chickpeas with 125 gm vegetables (about 1.5 cups)
Alice: Mediterranean chickpea, cucumber and tomato salad — 80 gm chickpeas with 100 gm vegetables (about 1 cup)
Joy: Garlic roasted broccoli — 150 gm (about 1.5 cups)
Alice: Garlic roasted broccoli — 120 gm (about 1.25 cups)
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 560 kcal, 45.0 g protein
Alice: 410 kcal, 34.0 g protein

Lemon-Yogurt Baked Chicken with Moong Dal, Spinach and Kachumber Salad (Indian)

🛒 Ingredients
• Chicken breast, skinless — 210 gm
• Plain unsweetened yogurt — 90 gm
• Lemon — x1
• Garlic cloves — x3
• Fresh ginger — 10 gm
• Paprika — 3 gm
• Turmeric — 2 gm
• Cumin seeds — 2 gm
• Split yellow moong dal (dry) — 80 gm
• Brown rice (dry) — 40 gm
• Fresh spinach — 200 gm
• Tomato — x2
• Cucumber — x1
• Red onion — x1
• Olive oil — 20 gm
• Fresh cilantro — 10 gm
• Salt — 1.5 gm
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 220C (425F). Bake the marinated chicken on a lined tray for 20-22 minutes until it reaches 74C (165F). Rest 5 minutes.
2. Simmer the dal with the remaining turmeric and 3 cups water for 20 minutes until soft. Cook the rice separately in 100 ml water for about 30 minutes.
3. Make the tadka: heat 10 gm oil, pop the cumin, add the remaining garlic and half the onion. Pour over the dal, add the remaining salt and stir.
4. Sauté the spinach in 5 gm oil for 2-3 minutes until wilted.
5. Toss the tomato, cucumber, remaining onion and cilantro with the remaining lemon juice and 5 gm oil.
6. Slice the chicken and plate it with the dal, rice, spinach and salad, using each diner's portion sizes.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Lemon-yogurt baked chicken — 120 gm raw (about 90 gm cooked)
Alice: Lemon-yogurt baked chicken — 90 gm raw (about 68 gm cooked)
Joy: Moong dal tadka — 1 cup cooked (45 gm dry)
Alice: Moong dal tadka — 3/4 cup cooked (35 gm dry)
Joy: Brown rice — 1/3 cup cooked
Alice: Brown rice — 1/4 cup cooked
Joy: Garlic sautéed spinach — 100 gm raw
Alice: Garlic sautéed spinach — 100 gm raw
Joy: Kachumber salad with lemon — 1 cup
Alice: Kachumber salad with lemon — 1 cup
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 570 kcal, 44.0 g protein
Alice: 425 kcal, 35.0 g protein

Tandoori-Style Chicken with Masoor Dal, Brown Rice and Kachumber Salad

🛒 Ingredients
• Chicken breast, skinless — 180 gm
• Plain unsweetened low-fat yogurt — 60 gm
• Red lentils (masoor dal), dry — 100 gm
• Brown rice, dry — 65 gm
• Cucumber — x1
• Tomato — x1
• Carrot — x1
• Garlic cloves — x3
• Fresh ginger — x1 inch piece
• Lemon — x1
• Olive or canola oil — 17 gm
• Cumin seeds — 1 tsp
• Turmeric powder — 0.5 tsp
• Garam masala — 1 tsp
• Chili powder — 0.5 tsp
• Salt — 0.33 tsp
• Fresh cilantro — 2 tbsp
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 220C (425F). Bake the marinated chicken on a lined tray for 18-20 minutes, until it reaches 74C (165F) inside. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
2. Boil the lentils with 2 cups water and the turmeric for 15-18 minutes, until soft. Mash lightly.
3. Tadka: heat the oil, then add the cumin seeds, remaining garlic and ginger. Cook for 1 minute, then stir into the dal with the remaining salt. Add a squeeze of lemon.
4. Reheat the rice. Dice the cucumber, tomato and carrot, then toss with the remaining lemon juice, cilantro and a pinch of chili powder. Do not add sugar or extra salt.
5. Plate according to each diner's portion sizes. Joy gets the larger portions of chicken, dal and rice.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Tandoori-style baked chicken — 100 gm raw weight (about 75 gm cooked)
Alice: Tandoori-style baked chicken — 80 gm raw weight (about 60 gm cooked)
Joy: Masoor dal — 0.75 cup cooked
Alice: Masoor dal — 0.5 cup cooked
Joy: Brown rice — 0.5 cup cooked
Alice: Brown rice — 0.33 cup cooked
Joy: Kachumber salad (cucumber, tomato, carrot, lemon) — 1.5 cups
Alice: Kachumber salad (cucumber, tomato, carrot, lemon) — 1.5 cups
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 560 kcal, 36.0 g protein
Alice: 410 kcal, 26.0 g protein

Tandoori Chicken with Moong Dal, Spinach-Cauliflower Sabzi and Cucumber Raita (Indian)

🛒 Ingredients
• Chicken breast, skinless — 240 gm
• Plain unsweetened Greek yogurt (low-fat) — 200 gm
• Dried yellow moong dal — 100 gm
• Brown rice, dry — 50 gm
• Cauliflower florets — 300 gm
• Fresh spinach — 200 gm
• Cucumber — x1
• Tomato — x2
• Onion — x1
• Garlic cloves — x4
• Fresh ginger — 20 gm
• Olive oil — 28 gm
• Cumin seeds — 2 gm
• Turmeric powder — 3 gm
• Garam masala — 3 gm
• Kashmiri chili powder — 3 gm
• Lemon — x1
• Fresh cilantro — 15 gm
• Salt — 2 gm
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 220C (425F). Marinate the chicken in 100 gm yogurt, half the ginger-garlic paste, the spice mix, lemon juice and 1 gm salt for 20 minutes.
2. Rinse the moong dal. Simmer in 3 cups water with the turmeric for 20 minutes until soft.
3. Bake the chicken on a lined tray for 20 to 25 minutes until it reaches 74C (165F) inside.
4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Fry the cumin seeds, sliced onion and the remaining ginger-garlic paste for 3 minutes. Add the cauliflower and 1/4 cup water, cover and cook for 8 minutes. Stir in the spinach until wilted and add a pinch of salt.
5. Temper the dal. Heat 1 tbsp oil, fry a pinch of cumin and 1 diced tomato for 2 minutes, then stir into the dal with 0.5 gm salt.
6. Make the raita. Mix 100 gm yogurt with diced cucumber, 1 diced tomato, cilantro and a squeeze of lemon.
7. Reheat the rice. Plate each diner's portions per the serving sizes and serve.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Tandoori-style baked chicken — 140 gm raw (about 105 gm cooked)
Alice: Tandoori-style baked chicken — 100 gm raw (about 75 gm cooked)
Joy: Moong dal — 1 cup cooked
Alice: Moong dal — 3/4 cup cooked
Joy: Spinach-cauliflower sabzi — 1.5 cups
Alice: Spinach-cauliflower sabzi — 1 cup
Joy: Cucumber-tomato raita — 3/4 cup
Alice: Cucumber-tomato raita — 1/2 cup
Joy: Brown rice — 1/2 cup cooked
Alice: Brown rice — 1/3 cup cooked
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 600 kcal, 34.0 g protein
Alice: 450 kcal, 26.0 g protein

Lemon-Herb Baked Chicken with Quinoa-Chickpea Mediterranean Salad

🛒 Ingredients
• Chicken breast, boneless skinless — 150 gm
• Quinoa, dry — 55 gm
• Chickpeas, canned low-sodium, rinsed — 140 gm
• Zucchini — 250 gm
• Cherry tomatoes — 150 gm
• Cucumber — 100 gm
• Plain unsweetened nonfat Greek yogurt — 90 gm
• Extra virgin olive oil — 25 gm
• Lemon — x1
• Garlic cloves — x2
• Dried oregano — 2 gm
• Fresh parsley — 10 gm
• Salt — 1.5 gm
• Black pepper — 1 gm
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat oven to 220C (425F). Place chicken on one side of a lined tray and zucchini tossed with 5 gm oil, pinch of salt and pepper on the other.

4. Mix yogurt with a little lemon zest, remaining garlic and pepper to make a sauce.
5. Plate the portions: Joy gets the larger salad and chicken portions, Alice the smaller ones. Top the zucchini with yogurt sauce and serve.

Joy: Lemon-herb baked chicken — 80 gm raw (about 60 gm cooked)
Alice: Lemon-herb baked chicken — 70 gm raw (about 52 gm cooked)
Joy: Quinoa-chickpea salad with tomato, cucumber and parsley — 1 cup (30 gm dry quinoa, 80 gm chickpeas)
Alice: Quinoa-chickpea salad with tomato, cucumber and parsley — 3/4 cup (25 gm dry quinoa, 60 gm chickpeas)
Joy: Roasted garlic zucchini with yogurt-lemon sauce — 1 cup zucchini with 50 gm yogurt
Alice: Roasted garlic zucchini with yogurt-lemon sauce — 3/4 cup zucchini with 40 gm yogurt
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 550 kcal, 37.0 g protein
Alice: 425 kcal, 31.0 g protein

Lemon-Herb Baked Salmon with Chickpea-Quinoa Salad and Roasted Broccoli (Mediterranean)

🛒 Ingredients
• Salmon fillet, skinless — 185 gm
• Quinoa, dry — 0.25 cups
• Low-sodium canned chickpeas, rinsed — 1 cups
• Broccoli florets — 2 cups
• Cucumber, small — x1
• Cherry tomatoes — 1 cups
• Lemon — x1
• Extra virgin olive oil — 3 tsp
• Garlic cloves — x2
• Dried oregano — 1 tsp
• Fresh parsley, chopped — 0.25 cups
• Salt — 0.25 tsp
• Black pepper — 0.5 tsp
👨‍🍳 Steps
1. Preheat oven to 200C (400F). Line a tray with parchment.
2. Toss broccoli with 1 tsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Roast 20 minutes, turning once.
3. Coat salmon with marinade and lemon slices. Bake alongside broccoli for 12-15 minutes until it flakes.
4. Chop cucumber and tomatoes. Toss with chickpeas, quinoa, parsley, remaining oil, juice of half a lemon, and remaining salt.
5. Plate each diner's portions as listed. Squeeze fresh lemon over the salmon and serve.
🍽 Servings
Joy: Lemon-herb baked salmon — 100 gm
Alice: Lemon-herb baked salmon — 85 gm
Joy: Chickpea-quinoa cucumber tomato salad — 1/3 cup quinoa, 1/2 cup chickpeas, 1 cup cucumber-tomato
Alice: Chickpea-quinoa cucumber tomato salad — 1/4 cup quinoa, 1/3 cup chickpeas, 3/4 cup cucumber-tomato
Joy: Garlic roasted broccoli — 1 cup
Alice: Garlic roasted broccoli — 3/4 cup
📊 Nutrition
Joy: 570 kcal, 34.0 g protein
Alice: 430 kcal, 26.0 g protein
